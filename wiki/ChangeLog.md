### 0.x.30

Added Yuki-Onna, Cecaelia, Mermaid and Valkyrie.

Added configs to tweak Gaia Features:

- `explosiveProjectileDestroysItems`: if false, Gaia's explosive projectiles will not destroy items on explosion. Default: `true`
- `spawnsMaleMobs`: if false, Gaia mobs' male variants will not spawn. Default: `true`
- `cecaeliaSpawnRate`: 0-1 double. Cecaelia spawn probability will be multiplied with this value.
- `allowsDayHostileMobSpawnOnGround`: if false, hostile Gaia mob will not spawn on the ground in daytime. The mob list can be configured with tag `nffgirlsgaia:can_disable_day_spawn`. Siren and Naga by default.

Disabled Gaia's original friending mechanic. Monster Feed and Premium Monster Feed will provide XP for NFF friended mobs instead.

Fixed AI issues related to unexpected goal addition by Gaia's dynamic goal mechanic.

Fixed missing some conversion recipes with Evil Grindstone.
