To get friendly with a Yuki-Onna, you need to give them items while resisting the horrible snowstorm.
Their surrounding snowstorm will cause a high level of Slowness as well as consistent damage to any creatures inside,
and can also block your view. So, get well-prepared before entering the storm.
<NEWLINE>
They love the Golden Apple, Golden Tropical Fish and HMaG berries. It also receives the Diamond, Emerald, Lapis Lazuli and Blue Ice.
<NEWLINE>
After friending, if you equip them a fan, the Yuki-Onna will be able to fight with powerful snowstorm magic.
<NEWLINE>
The Yuki-Onna can be healed with Snowball, Snow Block and Pumpkin Pie. A Golden Apple can fully recover the health.
