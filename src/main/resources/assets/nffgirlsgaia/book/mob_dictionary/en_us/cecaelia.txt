To get friendly with a Cecaelia, you need to give them items while staying with them in the water.
During the process, they will keep summoning vortexes, which will greatly disturb your actions.
They love the Nautilus Shell and Heart of the Sea, and also receives cooked fishes.
<NEWLINE>
The Cecaelia can be healed with cooked fishes and Golden Carrot, and Dried Kelp can heal a little bit.
A Golden Apple or Golden Tropical Fish can fully recover the health.
