To get friendly with a Mermaid, you need to give them items while staying with them in the water.
During the process, they will keep summoning vortexes, which will greatly disturb your actions.

<NEWLINE>
The Mermaid can be healed with cooked fishes and Golden Carrot, and Dried Kelp can heal a little bit.
A Golden Apple or Golden Tropical Fish can fully recover the health.
