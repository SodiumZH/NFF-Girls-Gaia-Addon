To get friendly with a Witch, you must first give them 5 different positive effects without
hurting them, before giving items.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Witch can either walk or fly (with a Broom on the offhand). In combat,
they can attack the enemies and support allies with different splash potions. The strengths of
the potion effects depend on their attributes. Attack Damage attribute boosts
the healing/damage amount and effect duration, while Poison Aspect attribute (provided by baubles)
boosts the effect level (both positive and negative).
<NEWLINE>
In addition, when you're drowning, burning or in the darkness, they will also throw
the corresponding potion to help you out.
