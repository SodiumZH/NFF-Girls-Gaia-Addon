To get friendly with a Werecat, you need to give them items by right-clicking. Accepted items are
shown in the JEI tab.
<NEWLINE>
As the progress increases, they will get Strength effect. Each 20% progress will
provide 1 level of the effect. Take extra care when you're almost done!
